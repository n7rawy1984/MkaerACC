## Settlement scope and rules

Applicable category: verified **foreign full-time monthly-paid employees under the traditional UAE private-sector scheme**. Uses existing11021/30-day gratuity bands, wage ceiling and disclosed ACTUAL_365 service convention. [Current MOHRE consolidated employment law and implementing rules](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/assets/download/fbb9bba2/Federal%20Decree-Law%20No.%2033%20of%202021%20Regarding%20the%20Regulation%20of%20Employment%20Relationship%20and%20its%20amendments_638990571068264034.pdf.aspx) distinguish termination leave cash-out on basic wage (Article29), applicable employer notice compensation on full wage (Article43), gratuity (Article51), final dues (Article53) and lawful recovery conditions. Applicability and supporting facts remain an authorized professional determination.

* All live posted salary liabilities through the termination month transfer once. A final month not already posted uses the **same canonical employee-scoped attendance/payroll helper**, including its approved Company scheduled-minute policy. Complete attendance and dated termination are required; unresolved drafts/future payroll block rather than double-recognize salary. This policy is disclosed, not presented as a universal statutory day-count rule.
* Unused leave is an evidenced approved balance entered in hundredths of days, calculated on last basic wage/30. This is not an automatic annual-leave entitlement ledger. Employer-owed **unserved** notice uses last full fixed wage/30; served notice remains payroll. Actual notice entitlement and unpaid service days require evidence.
* Known canonical gratuity payments reduce entitlement once. Other historical payments need separate references. Existing employee gratuity provision transfers; only the shortfall is newly expensed. Excess provisions/overpayments require separate correction. No IAS19 actuarial calculation is claimed.
* Approved already-accrued employee reimbursements transfer their unpaid liability; they create no second Project expense. Existing employee advances offset only with lawful authority. Other deductions require an evidenced existing dedicated employee receivable; partial offsets preserve its remainder. Dimensional other-deduction receivables require their original workflow rather than guessed allocations.
* Original liabilities/receivables are cleared in their actual GL Project/Contract dimensions. New final-month salary uses existing cost allocation; leave, notice and gratuity top-up use explicitly configured expense accounts. Final payable is a dedicated Party-required liability. No account or Company configuration is invented by migration or UI.

Workflow: select employee → review facts/source balances → preview → save editable draft → Accounting Admin approval → canonical post → authorized partial/final actual Treasury payment → statement/reconcile. Admin maps existing approved dedicated accounts with account-management permission. Accountant may prepare/download/pay within existing explicit permissions. Management Viewer, nonmembers and Platform Owner without Company payroll authority remain denied.

Accrual debits transferred liabilities/new entitlements and credits advance/authorized receivable offsets plus final payable. Payment debits final payable and credits actual Treasury. Every payment checks exact unpaid-control reconciliation, dated recorded funds and period controls. No duplicated payroll/gratuity/Project expense. Zero net closes without fictitious cash. Negative settlements, disputed deductions, pensions, alternative schemes, part-time/domestic/other jurisdictions, automated statutory eligibility/leave accrual and employer claims outside supported lawful receivables remain unsupported.

Posted history is immutable. Actual returned funds/payment correction uses canonical reversal; accrual reversal requires all live payments reversed. Source salary/advance/reimbursement reversals cannot invalidate a live settlement; final-month salary cannot also be posted separately. A corrected settlement uses a **new draft referencing the reversed settlement**, preserving both audit histories. Posting cannot precede linked financial effects. Version checks, actor-bound request hashing, shared Company locks and atomic document/journal commits prevent duplicates/races.
