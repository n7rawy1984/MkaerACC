## Business behavior and accounting

Before posting, an authorized accountant can choose Operating Expense, Project Cost, contractor payment/advance, Employee advance, Custody advance/return/settlement, Treasury, Supplier payment/invoice, Payroll or other existing financial-evidence destination. The command locks the source, cancels existing unposted83/85 claims through their canonical cancellation commands, retains their immutable events and updates source review metadata. Original raw records, amount, date, file/attachment metadata and prior audit remain. Source review resets to PENDING; routing itself posts no journal and creates no replacement financial document. Existing workflows remain responsible for evidence, approved master/dimension selection, review and posting. Treasury/other-evidence routing deliberately returns to NEEDS_REVIEW rather than inventing a transfer, inflow, partner transaction or missing command. Split/archived/posted claims cannot be bypassed through routing.

After posting, a source-linked whole-document correction follows DRAFT → REVIEWED → APPROVED → POSTED. ACCOUNTANT can prepare/review authorized plans; the existing Accounting Admin correction authority approves/executes. No new mandatory second operator or managerial role is introduced. The correction date is server today in the existing UTC correction-period policy; the operator must explicitly confirm the current Company correction period OPEN through the existing command. CLOSED remains terminal. Review/posting recompute authority, source/document state and policy; changed source/version invalidates the saved plan. Explicit no-tax-adjustment/no-recoverable-VAT confirmation is required; a payment amount never establishes replacement tax treatment. Amount and actual original funding location are derived from the canonical document, never editable replacement inputs.

Supported origins/destinations reuse the actual canonical Expense, subcontractor payment/advance, Employee advance, Custody advance and Supplier-payment commands. Operating/Project costs share Expense. Existing certified/supplier liabilities must exist; correction never creates a certificate, invoice, account, opening or balancing amount. Existing Project allocation/reclassification-entry and specialized Payroll correction workflows remain available; this full-document correction does not replace them or relax their scope.

For an erroneous **AED5,000 Operating Expense identified as a contractor payment**, the canonical Expense reversal removes the erroneous cost and an existing-liability subcontractor payment clears AED5,000 payable. Both commands execute in one transaction, with the same original Treasury and exact amount. Original journal lines remain immutable; the original document receives only its canonical reversal transition. The replacement has an explicit RECLASSIFICATION reference and the event links original/reversal/replacement journals. A failed or overallocated replacement rolls back its reversal and every financial effect.

**Funding neutrality is enforced on the actual pair of correction journals**, grouped by account, Treasury and (for Custody-funded cost corrections) accountable holder. Every original funding group must net to zero; a different funding location/holder or extra outflow causes rollback. This does not depend on unrelated concurrent ledger movement. Reversal and replacement contain visible, balanced accounting movements; they are not an actual refund and new disbursement. Their receipt states `actual_new_payment=false`, zero funding delta and retains the first physical payment date/journal across later corrections. Ledger gross debit/credit movement must not be mistaken for new physical cash activity. The original actual payment evidence is retained separately from the correction accounting date.

New Expense→advance treatment creates a receivable/asset, not another work/payroll cost. Employee advances keep existing HR mapping/recovery limits. Existing recovery/certificate/settlement dependencies prevent unsafe reversal of consumed advances; no dependent record is rewritten. Custody-funded cost corrections retain the same funding holder and balance; cross-module Custody funding requiring an unsupported Treasury fiction is rejected. Inactive/closed-master settlement rules remain those of the existing canonical commands.

## Supported scope and remaining policy gates

Full-document execution is limited to current-UTC-period originals, today's explicitly open correction period, zero VAT, unsplit documents and verified unreconciled classification errors. This is an explicit bounded policy, not a new general fiscal close or prior-period restatement policy. Tax-bearing, prior/closed-period, reconciled, split-allocation and dependent consumed-document cases remain blocked/DECISION REQUIRED or use their existing specialized workflow. Payroll accrual/payment/recovery corrections retain their canonical payroll flow; no generic salary command is introduced. Personal/supplier-credit Expense recognition requiring liability-specific correction is not forced into cash. One selected certified/Supplier liability is allocated per replacement payment; broader matching remains future scope. Unsupported Treasury transfers/funding variants are not invented by destination routing.

Supported local accounting behavior is complete for this scope; this does not claim every historical transaction or commercial ERP policy is now executable.
